# "INSTITUTIONALIZED" — Animated TikTok Series Plan

**Source:** Christina Wood's short story "INSTITUTIONALIZED" (ENG 226 final, Sept 2026)
**Format:** 6 episodes, 30–60 seconds each, vertical 1080×1920
**Tone:** Moody, cinematic, emotional — NOT cartoonish. Dark burgundy palette, gold accents, film-grain texture. The animation honors the story; the story is deeply personal.
**Style:** Pure cinematic illustrated scenes + dark text cards. Painterly, desaturated, dark burgundy grade. Slow fades, text overlays from the actual prose. NO brand-girl animations (removed per Christina's feedback, 2026-10-10 — they clashed with the story's tone).
**Status:** ✅ All 7 episodes BUILT. Episode 1 v2 approved by Christina 2026-10-10. Episodes 2–6 + prequel built 2026-10-10 per the approved framework with escalating craft (per-episode grade moods, new signature scenes, evolved card layouts, motif reuse for continuity). NOTHING POSTED without Christina's explicit go-ahead.

## Production notes
- **Palette:** Dark burgundy base (#1A080D / #2A0A12), champagne gold (#C9A96E) text/accents, cream (#F7E7CE) for emphasis lines. Desaturated vs. the bright cute-luxe TikToks.
- **Typography:** Playfair Display (headlines), Pinyon/Parisienne script (emotional beats), Montserrat (body). Same font files as main TikToks.
- **Transitions:** Slow fades and gentle radial wipes — cinematic, not whip pans. Ken Burns drift slower than brand TikToks.
- **Animation:** Cinematic AI-generated illustrated scenes only (assets/, 1280x720) — moody, painterly, desaturated, dark burgundy. Brand-girl clips are NOT used in this series (removed per Christina's feedback 2026-10-10).
- **Audio:** No baked audio — add trending/moody sounds in-app at post time. Suggested: dark cinematic trending audio, or a soft piano trend.
- **Captions:** Each episode gets a caption with hook + #institutionalized #shesthevibe #womenwriters #healingjourney + "Part X" labeling.
- **Approval gate:** Christina previews Episode 1. Only after approval do Episodes 2–6 + the prequel get built. Nothing is posted without her explicit go-ahead per episode or per series.
