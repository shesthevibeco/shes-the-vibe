### 1. SERVICES

Coach provides one-on-one life coaching services ("Services") in the following tracks. Client and Coach will select the track (or combination) that best fits Client's needs during the discovery process:

- **UNTANGLED — Divorce & Separation Support.** Coaching for navigating divorce, separation, and co-parenting with clarity and self-trust. Emotional support and practical strategy — not legal advice.
- **CYCLE BREAKER — Breaking Generational Cycles.** Coaching for identifying inherited patterns, healing old wounds, and building a different way forward for yourself and your family.
- **SOFT RESET — Burnout Recovery.** Coaching for recovering from burnout, rebuilding energy, and designing a sustainable rhythm that doesn't run you into the ground.

Coaching is a collaborative, forward-focused process. Coach will use conversation, reflective exercises, goal-setting, and accountability structures tailored to Client's track and goals.

**Coaching is not therapy, counseling, or medical treatment.** See Section 9.

### 2. SESSION STRUCTURE

- **Format:** All sessions are conducted via live video call unless otherwise agreed in writing.
- **Length:** Standard sessions are sixty (60) minutes. Discovery calls are twenty (20) minutes and complimentary.
- **What's included in each package:**
  - Live coaching sessions per the selected package
  - Written session recap + worksheets delivered within 24 hours of each session
  - Between-session support via text or voice message, Monday–Friday (Coach responds within 24 hours)
  - 8-session packages additionally include one (1) complimentary 30-minute check-in call within 30 days of package completion

**Package timelines** (to protect momentum):
- Single sessions: to be completed within 30 days of purchase
- 4-session packages: to be completed within 6 weeks of the first session
- 8-session packages: to be completed within 12 weeks of the first session

Sessions not completed within these windows expire without refund, unless an extension is agreed in writing. Life happens — if you need more time, just talk to Coach before the window closes.

### 4. RESCHEDULING POLICY

Life happens — on both sides. Coach extends the same grace she needs.

- **More than 24 hours' notice:** Reschedule free, no questions asked. Just rebook.
- **Less than 24 hours' notice:** The three-strike system applies (to both Parties):
  - **Strikes 1 and 2:** Grace. No penalty. Kids get sick, dogs have emergencies, things come up.
  - **Strike 3 — Client cancels:** The session counts as used. It is treated as a missed session and is non-refundable.
  - **Strike 3 — Coach cancels:** Client receives one (1) complimentary session added to their package.
- Strikes reset at the start of each new package.
- **No-shows:** A session missed without any communication counts as a strike. Client agrees to communicate — a quick text is all it takes. Communication goes both ways.

---

### 5. EARLY TERMINATION & REFUNDS

Client may end a package early at any time by notifying Coach in writing (email is fine).

- **Before Session 1:** Full refund of all amounts paid. No questions asked.
- **After Session 1 but before Session 2:** Refund of remaining balance, minus (a) the cost of Session 1 billed at the single-session rate for Client's track, and (b) a $75 administrative fee.
- **After Session 2:** Completed sessions are recalculated individually at the single-session rate for Client's track (the package discount no longer applies). Any remaining balance after this recalculation is refunded to Client. If the recalculation shows a balance owed, Client agrees to settle it within fourteen (14) days.

Package sessions may be transferred to another person only with Coach's prior written approval.

---

### 6. COACH'S RIGHT TO TERMINATE

Coach reserves the right to discontinue the coaching relationship at any time, at Coach's sole discretion, including but not limited to situations involving abusive, racist, threatening, harassing, or otherwise unsafe behavior toward Coach.

If Coach terminates under this section:
- Coach will communicate the reason directly to Client.
- Client will be refunded according to the early-termination calculation in Section 5 (sessions recalculated at the single-session rate).
- This provision exists to keep coaching a safe, respectful space for everyone.

---

### 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

Coach agrees to keep all information shared by Client during coaching strictly confidential and will not disclose it to any third party without Client's prior written consent, **except** as required in Section 8 (Mandated Reporting) or by valid legal process (such as a court order or subpoena).

Client agrees to keep Coach's proprietary materials — including worksheets, frameworks, exercises, and program content — for personal use only and not to reproduce, distribute, or share them without Coach's written permission.

**Note:** Video sessions and messages are not conducted on HIPAA-covered platforms. Coach is not a covered entity under HIPAA. Coach nonetheless treats Client information with the seriousness it deserves.

### 8. MANDATED REPORTER DISCLOSURE

**Coach is a mandated reporter in the state of California.** This means that if, in the course of coaching, Coach learns of or reasonably suspects:

- Child abuse or neglect,
- Elder or dependent adult abuse or neglect, or
- A threat of serious physical harm to Client or another identifiable person,

Coach is **legally required** to report it to the appropriate authorities (such as Child Protective Services, Adult Protective Services, or law enforcement), even without Client's consent.

Client acknowledges this disclosure by signing below. Everything else shared in coaching remains confidential as described in Section 7. Coach will be upfront with Client if a reporting obligation ever arises.

### 9. COACHING VS. THERAPY — IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER

- Christina Wood is a **certified life coach** (Life Coaching + NLP Practitioner) and a **Certified Positive Parenting Coach**. She is **not a licensed therapist, psychologist, counselor, or medical professional**.
- Coaching is **not** a substitute for therapy, counseling, psychiatric care, or medical treatment. Coaching does not diagnose or treat mental health conditions.
- Coach does not provide legal advice. Divorce & Separation Support coaching addresses the emotional and practical dimensions of the process; it is not legal counsel, and Client is encouraged to consult a licensed attorney for legal matters.
- If Client is currently in therapy or under medical care, Client agrees to consult their provider about adding coaching, and to inform Coach.
- If, during coaching, Coach believes Client would be better served by a licensed professional, Coach will say so honestly and may refer Client accordingly.
