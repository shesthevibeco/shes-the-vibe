### 1. WHAT COACHING IS

Life coaching is a collaborative, forward-focused partnership. Together, we will:

- Clarify where you are now and where you want to be
- Identify patterns, beliefs, or habits that may be holding you back
- Set meaningful, realistic goals
- Build practical strategies and action steps
- Create accountability so momentum doesn't fizzle out

Coaching draws on conversation, reflective exercises, and proven frameworks. It is action-oriented and centered on your growth, in your words, on your terms.

---

### 2. WHAT COACHING IS NOT

Please read this carefully — it matters:

- **Coaching is not therapy.** Christina Wood is a certified life coach (Life Coaching + NLP Practitioner) and Certified Positive Parenting Coach. She is **not** a licensed therapist, psychologist, counselor, or medical professional.
- **Coaching does not diagnose or treat** mental health conditions, including depression, anxiety, PTSD, eating disorders, or any other clinical condition.
- **Coaching is not a substitute** for medical care, psychiatric care, or psychotherapy.
- **Coaching is not legal advice.** If your track touches on divorce or separation, coaching addresses the emotional and practical dimensions only. Consult a licensed attorney for legal matters.
- **Coaching is not crisis intervention.** If you are in crisis, see Section 7.

If you are currently working with a therapist, psychiatrist, or medical provider, you are encouraged to discuss adding coaching with them.

---

### 3. VOLUNTARY PARTICIPATION

Your participation in coaching is **entirely voluntary**. You are choosing to be here, and that choice matters.

- You may ask questions at any time — about the process, the methods, or anything else.
- You may decline to answer any question or skip any exercise. No explanation required.
- You may end the coaching relationship at any time (see the Coaching Agreement for refund terms).

---

### 4. BENEFITS AND RISKS

**Potential benefits** of coaching include greater self-awareness, clearer goals, improved coping strategies, stronger boundaries, better communication, and meaningful progress toward the life you want.

**Potential risks** — coaching isn't always comfortable:
- You may experience difficult emotions (sadness, anger, grief, frustration) as we explore honest topics.
- You may feel temporarily unsettled as old patterns are examined.
- Insights from coaching can affect relationships as you change how you show up in them.
- Progress isn't always linear. Some weeks will feel like breakthroughs; others like standing still. Both are normal.

Coach will hold the space with care, but cannot eliminate all discomfort from growth.

### 5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND ITS LIMITS

Everything you share in coaching is confidential and will not be disclosed without your written consent, **except** in the following circumstances:

1. **Mandated reporting (California):** Coach is a mandated reporter. If Coach learns of or reasonably suspects child abuse or neglect, elder or dependent adult abuse, or a threat of serious physical harm to you or another identifiable person, Coach is legally required to report it to the appropriate authorities — even without your consent. **Please initial here to acknowledge you understand: ______**
2. **Legal process:** If ordered by a court (subpoena, court order), Coach may be compelled to disclose information.
3. **Consultation:** Coach may discuss cases in general, de-identified terms with professional mentors or supervisors for quality purposes. Your identity will never be revealed.

### 6. COMMUNICATION BETWEEN SESSIONS

- Between-session support is available via text or voice message, **Monday–Friday**. Coach responds within 24 hours.
- This support is for coaching continuity — reflections, quick questions, accountability check-ins.
- **It is not a crisis line.** If you are in crisis, use the resources in Section 7, not text/voice messaging.

---

### 7. EMERGENCY PROCEDURES

**Coaching is not crisis support.** If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, thoughts of harming yourself or others, or any urgent safety concern:

- **Call or text 988** — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (24/7, free, confidential)
- **Text HOME to 741741** — Crisis Text Line (24/7)
- **Call 911** — if there is immediate danger
- **Go to your nearest emergency room**

Coach is not available for crisis intervention and cannot guarantee immediate response outside of scheduled sessions.

---

### 8. RIGHT TO WITHDRAW

You may withdraw from coaching at any time, for any reason, without penalty beyond the refund terms described in the Coaching Agreement. If you choose to withdraw, Coach will provide referrals to other resources if you'd like them.
