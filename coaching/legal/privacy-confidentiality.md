# Privacy & Confidentiality Policy

> **A note from Christina:** *What you tell me matters, and how I handle it matters too. This is my promise to you about your information — what I collect, why, who sees it (basically no one), and your rights. Plain language, no fine-print games.* 💛

## SHE'S THE VIBE CO — PRIVACY & CONFIDENTIALITY POLICY

**Effective date:** The date Client signs the Coaching Agreement.

**Contact for privacy questions:** Christina Wood — christina@shesthevibe.co

---

### 1. WHAT INFORMATION IS COLLECTED

To provide coaching services, Coach collects:

- **Contact information:** Name, email, phone number, city/state
- **Intake information:** Responses on the Intake Form (goals, background, preferences, emergency contact)
- **Session records:** Coach's private session notes, action plans, and recaps shared with Client
- **Communications:** Emails, text/voice messages exchanged between sessions
- **Payment information:** Billing details processed through Coach's secure payment processor (Coach does not store full card numbers)

Coach collects only what is needed to deliver coaching safely and well.

---

### 2. HOW INFORMATION IS STORED

- **Digital records** (intake forms, session notes, communications) are stored in password-protected accounts and devices accessible only to Coach.
- **Payment data** is handled by Coach's payment processor (e.g., Stripe) under that processor's own security standards. Coach does not retain full payment card numbers.
- **Physical records**, if any, are kept in a locked location accessible only to Coach.
- Coach takes reasonable steps to protect Client information from unauthorized access, but no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

---

### 3. WHO HAS ACCESS

**Only Christina Wood** has access to Client's coaching records in the ordinary course of business.

Client information is **never** sold, rented, or shared for marketing purposes. Period.

Limited exceptions where information may be accessed or disclosed:

1. **Mandated reporting (California):** As a mandated reporter, Coach is legally required to report suspected child abuse/neglect, elder or dependent adult abuse, or threats of serious physical harm to the appropriate authorities — even without Client's consent.
2. **Legal process:** If compelled by a valid court order, subpoena, or other legal requirement.
3. **De-identified professional consultation:** Coach may discuss cases in general, anonymized terms with a professional mentor or supervisor. No identifying information is ever shared.
4. **Emergency:** If Coach has reason to believe Client is at imminent risk of serious harm, Coach may contact Client's emergency contact or emergency services.

---

### 4. VIDEO SESSIONS & MESSAGES

- Sessions are conducted via video conferencing. Coach uses reputable platforms but cannot guarantee the platform's own security practices — Clients are encouraged to review the platform's privacy policy.
- Between-session text/voice messages are part of the coaching record and treated with the same confidentiality.
- Coach will not record video sessions without Client's prior written consent. Client agrees not to record sessions without Coach's prior written consent.

---

### 5. DATA RETENTION

- **Active client records** are kept for the duration of the coaching relationship plus **seven (7) years** thereafter, consistent with standard professional record-keeping practices.
- **Payment records** are retained as required by tax and financial regulations.
- After the retention period, records are securely deleted or destroyed.
- If Client requests earlier deletion, Coach will comply to the extent permitted by legal and professional obligations (see Section 6).

---

### 6. CLIENT RIGHTS

Client has the right to:

- **Access:** Request a copy of the personal information Coach holds about them.
- **Correct:** Request correction of inaccurate information.
- **Delete:** Request deletion of personal information, subject to Coach's legal retention obligations.
- **Withdraw consent:** Withdraw from coaching at any time (see Coaching Agreement for financial terms).
- **Ask questions:** Contact christina@shesthevibe.co with any privacy concern, and receive a response within a reasonable time.

To exercise any of these rights, email christina@shesthevibe.co with the subject line "Privacy Request."

---

### 7. CALIFORNIA PRIVACY NOTE

California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and related laws, including the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to request deletion, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. This policy is intended to honor those rights. Coach does not sell personal information as defined by California law.

---

### 8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes materially, Coach will notify active and recent Clients by email. The current version will always be available upon request.
