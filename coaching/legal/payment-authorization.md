### 3. LATE PAYMENTS

- If any payment is more than **seven (7) days** past due, coaching sessions will **pause** until the account is brought current.
- Coach will send a reminder before sessions are paused. No shame — just boundaries.
- Balances more than thirty (30) days past due may be referred for collection, and Client agrees to be responsible for reasonable collection costs.

---

### 4. REFUND POLICY

Refunds are governed by the **Early Termination** terms in the Coaching Services Agreement (Section 5):

- **Before Session 1:** Full refund, no questions asked.
- **After Session 1, before Session 2:** Refund minus Session 1 at the single-session rate and a $75 admin fee.
- **After Session 2:** Completed sessions recalculated at the single-session rate (package discount removed); remaining balance refunded or settled.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within fourteen (14) business days of the refund determination.

---

### 5. FAILED / DECLINED PAYMENTS

- If a scheduled charge is declined, Coach will notify Client and attempt the charge again within 48 hours.
- A **$25 fee** may apply for each failed payment after the first occurrence, to cover processing costs.
- Two consecutive failed payments will pause the payment plan until resolved with Coach directly.
