### 4. RESCHEDULING POLICY

Life happens — on both sides. Coach extends the same grace she needs.

- **More than 24 hours' notice:** Reschedule free, no questions asked. Just rebook.
- **Less than 24 hours' notice:** The three-strike system applies (to both Parties):
  - **Strikes 1 and 2:** Grace. No penalty. Kids get sick, dogs have emergencies, things come up.
  - **Strike 3 — Client cancels:** The session counts as used. It is treated as a missed session and is non-refundable.
  - **Strike 3 — Coach cancels:** Client receives one (1) complimentary session added to their package.
- Strikes reset at the start of each new package.
- **No-shows:** A session missed without any communication counts as a strike. Client agrees to communicate — a quick text is all it takes. Communication goes both ways.

Any remaining balance after this recalculation is refunded to Client. If the recalculation shows a balance owed, Client agrees to settle it within fourteen (14) days.

Package sessions may be transferred to another person only with Coach's prior written approval.

## SHE'S THE VIBE CO — INFORMED CONSENT

**Coach:** Christina Wood, She's The Vibe Co (christina@shesthevibe.co)

---

### 1. WHAT COACHING IS

Life coaching is a collaborative, forward-focused partnership. Together, we will:

- Clarify where you are now and where you want to be
- Identify patterns, beliefs, or habits that may be holding you back
- Set meaningful, realistic goals
- Build practical strategies and action steps
- Create accountability so momentum doesn't fizzle out

Coaching draws on conversation, reflective exercises, and proven frameworks. It is action-oriented and centered on your growth, in your words, on your terms.

---

### 2. WHAT COACHING IS NOT

Please read this carefully — it matters:

- **Coaching is not therapy.** Christina Wood is a certified life coach (Life Coaching + NLP Practitioner) and Certified Positive Parenting Coach. She is **not** a licensed therapist, psychologist, counselor, or medical professional.
- **Coaching does not diagnose or treat** mental health conditions, including depression, anxiety, PTSD, eating disorders, or any other clinical condition.
- **Coaching is not a substitute** for medical care, psychiatric care, or psychotherapy.
- **Coaching is not legal advice.** If your track touches on divorce or separation, coaching addresses the emotional and practical dimensions only. Consult a licensed attorney for legal matters.
- **Coaching is not crisis intervention.** If you are in crisis, see Section 7.

If you are currently working with a therapist, psychiatrist, or medical provider, you are encouraged to discuss adding coaching with them.

---

### 3. VOLUNTARY PARTICIPATION

Your participation in coaching is **entirely voluntary**. You are choosing to be here, and that choice matters.

- You may ask questions at any time — about the process, the methods, or anything else.
- You may decline to answer any question or skip any exercise. No explanation required.
- You may end the coaching relationship at any time (see the Coaching Agreement for refund terms).

---

### 4. BENEFITS AND RISKS

**Potential benefits** of coaching include greater self-awareness, clearer goals, improved coping strategies, stronger boundaries, better communication, and meaningful progress toward the life you want.

**Potential risks** — coaching isn't always comfortable:
- You may experience difficult emotions (sadness, anger, grief, frustration) as we explore honest topics.
- You may feel temporarily unsettled as old patterns are examined.
- Insights from coaching can affect relationships as you change how you show up in them.
- Progress isn't always linear. Some weeks will feel like breakthroughs; others like standing still. Both are normal.

Coach will hold the space with care, but cannot eliminate all discomfort from growth.

---

### 5. CONFIDENTIALITY AND ITS LIMITS

Everything you share in coaching is confidential and will not be disclosed without your written consent, **except** in the following circumstances:

1. **Mandated reporting (California):** Coach is a mandated reporter. If Coach learns of or reasonably suspects child abuse or neglect, elder or dependent adult abuse, or a threat of serious physical harm to you or another identifiable person, Coach is legally required to report it to the appropriate authorities — even without your consent. **Please initial here to acknowledge you understand: ______**
2. **Legal process:** If ordered by a court (subpoena, court order), Coach may be compelled to disclose information.
3. **Consultation:** Coach may discuss cases in general, de-identified terms with professional mentors or supervisors for quality purposes. Your identity will never be revealed.

---

### 6. COMMUNICATION BETWEEN SESSIONS

- Between-session support is available via text or voice message, **Monday–Friday**. Coach responds within 24 hours.
- This support is for coaching continuity — reflections, quick questions, accountability check-ins.
- **It is not a crisis line.** If you are in crisis, use the resources in Section 7, not text/voice messaging.

---

### 7. EMERGENCY PROCEDURES

**Coaching is not crisis support.** If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, thoughts of harming yourself or others, or any urgent safety concern:

- **Call or text 988** — Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (24/7, free, confidential)
- **Text HOME to 741741** — Crisis Text Line (24/7)
- **Call 911** — if there is immediate danger
- **Go to your nearest emergency room**

Coach is not available for crisis intervention and cannot guarantee immediate response outside of scheduled sessions.

### 8. RIGHT TO WITHDRAW

You may withdraw from coaching at any time, for any reason, without penalty beyond the refund terms described in the Coaching Agreement. If you choose to withdraw, Coach will provide referrals to other resources if you'd like them.

# Part 3: Privacy & Confidentiality Policy

> **A note from Christina:** *What you tell me matters, and how I handle it matters too. This is my promise to you about your information — what I collect, why, who sees it (basically no one), and your rights. Plain language, no fine-print games.* 💛

## SHE'S THE VIBE CO — PRIVACY & CONFIDENTIALITY POLICY

**Effective date:** The date Client signs the Coaching Agreement.

**Contact for privacy questions:** Christina Wood — christina@shesthevibe.co

---

### 1. WHAT INFORMATION IS COLLECTED

To provide coaching services, Coach collects:

- **Contact information:** Name, email, phone number, city/state
- **Intake information:** Responses on the Intake Form (goals, background, preferences, emergency contact)
- **Session records:** Coach's private session notes, action plans, and recaps shared with Client
- **Communications:** Emails, text/voice messages exchanged between sessions
- **Payment information:** Billing details processed through Coach's secure payment processor (Coach does not store full card numbers)

Coach collects only what is needed to deliver coaching safely and well.

---

### 2. HOW INFORMATION IS STORED

- **Digital records** (intake forms, session notes, communications) are stored in password-protected accounts and devices accessible only to Coach.
- **Payment data** is handled by Coach's payment processor (e.g., Stripe) under that processor's own security standards. Coach does not retain full payment card numbers.
- **Physical records**, if any, are kept in a locked location accessible only to Coach.
- Coach takes reasonable steps to protect Client information from unauthorized access, but no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

---

### 3. WHO HAS ACCESS

**Only Christina Wood** has access to Client's coaching records in the ordinary course of business.

Client information is **never** sold, rented, or shared for marketing purposes. Period.

Limited exceptions where information may be accessed or disclosed:

1. **Mandated reporting (California):** As a mandated reporter, Coach is legally required to report suspected child abuse/neglect, elder or dependent adult abuse, or threats of serious physical harm to the appropriate authorities — even without Client's consent.
2. **Legal process:** If compelled by a valid court order, subpoena, or other legal requirement.
3. **De-identified professional consultation:** Coach may discuss cases in general, anonymized terms with a professional mentor or supervisor. No identifying information is ever shared.
4. **Emergency:** If Coach has reason to believe Client is at imminent risk of serious harm, Coach may contact Client's emergency contact or emergency services.

---

### 4. VIDEO SESSIONS & MESSAGES

- Sessions are conducted via video conferencing. Coach uses reputable platforms but cannot guarantee the platform's own security practices — Clients are encouraged to review the platform's privacy policy.
- Between-session text/voice messages are part of the coaching record and treated with the same confidentiality.
- Coach will not record video sessions without Client's prior written consent. Client agrees not to record sessions without Coach's prior written consent.

---

### 5. DATA RETENTION

- **Active client records** are kept for the duration of the coaching relationship plus **seven (7) years** thereafter, consistent with standard professional record-keeping practices.
- **Payment records** are retained as required by tax and financial regulations.
- After the retention period, records are securely deleted or destroyed.
- If Client requests earlier deletion, Coach will comply to the extent permitted by legal and professional obligations (see Section 6).

---

### 6. CLIENT RIGHTS

Client has the right to:

- **Access:** Request a copy of the personal information Coach holds about them.
- **Correct:** Request correction of inaccurate information.
- **Delete:** Request deletion of personal information, subject to Coach's legal retention obligations.
- **Withdraw consent:** Withdraw from coaching at any time (see Coaching Agreement for financial terms).
- **Ask questions:** Contact christina@shesthevibe.co with any privacy concern, and receive a response within a reasonable time.

To exercise any of these rights, email christina@shesthevibe.co with the subject line "Privacy Request."

---

### 7. CALIFORNIA PRIVACY NOTE

California residents have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and related laws, including the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to request deletion, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. This policy is intended to honor those rights. Coach does not sell personal information as defined by California law.

---

### 8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes materially, Coach will notify active and recent Clients by email. The current version will always be available upon request.
