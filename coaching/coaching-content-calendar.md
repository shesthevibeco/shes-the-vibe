## Content Format Variety

Rotate through these so the feed never goes stale:
- 📝 Text-based carousels (educational)
- 🎥 Talking-head Reels (personal stories, myth-busting)
- 💬 Quote graphics (inspirational one-liners)
- 📸 Behind-the-scenes (desk, prep, real life)
- 🔴 IG Lives (Q&As — save to highlights)
- 📊 Polls & question stickers (engagement + market research)
- 📖 Mini case studies (transformation stories)

## Repurposing

Every piece of coaching content should also become:
- A **blog post** (SEO — "divorce coach," "breaking generational cycles," "burnout recovery" are searchable terms)
- An **email** to the list (nurture sequence for future clients)
- A **Pinterest pin** (coaching searches are huge on Pinterest)

## Soft CTAs to Rotate

- "Link in bio to book a free discovery call 💛"
- "DM me 'READY' if this hit home"
- "Save this for when you need it"
- "Share with someone who needs to hear this"
- "Which track sounds like you? 👇"

---

*This calendar is a starting framework, not a cage. If something's trending or a client story is fresh, post that instead. Authenticity > schedule.*
