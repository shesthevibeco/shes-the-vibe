## The Vibe on Coaching

I'm not a therapist. I'm not going to sit across from you with a clipboard and nod silently. I'm a certified life coach (NLP practitioner + positive parenting coach) who has *lived* the hard stuff — messy divorce, courtrooms, breaking the cycles I was raised in, raising three kids while rebuilding my whole life.

Coaching with me is real talk. We get honest, we get practical, and we leave every session with something you can actually *do*. No shame, no judgment, no toxic positivity. Just hype for the person you're becoming.

**Everyone is welcome here.** All genders. All parents. Single, married, divorced, it's-complicated. If you're a human going through it, you belong.
