## The Three Coaching Tracks

### 🔀 UNTANGLED — Divorce & Separation Support
*You're not broken. You're just in the messy middle.*

For anyone navigating divorce, separation, or the aftermath — whether you're thinking about leaving, in the middle of it, or picking up the pieces. Especially when kids are involved and you're trying to keep them at the center of it all.

*Coaching is emotional support and strategy — not legal advice. For legal questions, talk to a lawyer.*

### 🔁 CYCLE BREAKER — Breaking Generational Cycles
*It ends with you. And it starts with you too.*

For anyone who looks at the patterns they grew up with — the yelling, the silence, the people-pleasing, the "just deal with it" energy — and thinks *not my kids, not my life, not anymore.*

### 🔋 SOFT RESET — Burnout Recovery
*You can't pour from an empty cup. Let's fill yours back up.*

For the overwhelmed. The "I'm fine" (you're not fine). If rest feels like a luxury you haven't earned and you're running on fumes — this is for you.

**Not sure which track?** That's normal — real life doesn't fit in neat boxes. Most of my clients touch on more than one. We'll figure out where to start together.

## Cancellation & Rescheduling — The Short Version

Life happens on both sides. Here's the deal:

- **More than 24 hours notice** → free reschedule, no questions asked
- **Under 24 hours** → three-strike system (goes both ways — I hold myself to the same standard)
  - Strikes 1 & 2: grace, no penalty
  - Strike 3 (you): the session counts as used
  - Strike 3 (me): you get a free session
- **No-call, no-shows** aren't okay — just send a quick text. Communication goes both ways.
- **Full refunds** available before your first session, no questions asked.

The full policy is in the Coaching Agreement you'll sign before we begin.
