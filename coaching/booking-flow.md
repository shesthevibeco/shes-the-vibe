# She's The Vibe — Coaching Booking Flow
*How it works, start to finish*

---

## The Journey: From Curious to Coached

### Step 1: Free Discovery Call (20 min)
**What it is:** A no-pressure vibe check. We talk about where you are, what you're dealing with, and whether coaching with me is the right fit. I'll be honest if I think you'd be better served elsewhere.

**How to book:** Link on the coaching page → pick a time → get a confirmation email with the video link.

**What happens:** We chat. You talk, I listen, I ask questions. By the end, you'll know which track fits (or if it's a mix), and I'll recommend a package. Zero pressure to decide on the spot.

---

### Step 2: Choose Your Package & Pay Your Deposit
**What it is:** You pick your track and tier (single, 4-pack, or 8-pack). To lock in your spot, you pay a deposit.

**Deposit structure:**

| Package | Total | Deposit to Book | When Balance Is Due |
|---------|-------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Single session | $150–175 | **$75** (or pay in full) | 48 hours before session |
| 4-session package | $547–597 | **50%** ($274–299) | 50% before Session 3 |
| 8-session package | $947–1,097 | **1st of 3 payments** (~$316–366) | Monthly (before sessions 4 and 7) |

**Why deposits?** Because your time matters and so does mine. A deposit means you're committed, I'm committed, and neither of us is flaking. It also keeps my calendar real — I only take a limited number of clients so everyone gets my full energy.

**How to pay:** Secure payment link (Stripe). All major cards accepted.

---

### Step 3: Intake Form
**What it is:** After your deposit, you'll get a short intake form. Nothing scary — just the basics so I can show up prepared.

**What I ask:**
- What brought you to coaching right now?
- What track are you most drawn to? (Untangled / Cycle Breaker / Soft Reset)
- What's the #1 thing you want to be different 3 months from now?
- Anything I should know? (Triggers, sensitivities, accessibility needs)
- How do you prefer to communicate between sessions? (Text / voice notes)

**Confidentiality:** Everything you share stays between us. Period.

---

### Step 4: Schedule Your Sessions
**What it is:** You get access to my booking calendar to schedule all your sessions upfront (recommended) or book as you go.

**Scheduling guidelines:**
- 4-session packages: complete within 6 weeks (weekly or biweekly)
- 8-session packages: complete within 12 weeks (weekly or biweekly)
- Single sessions: book within 30 days of purchase

**Why book ahead?** Momentum matters. Coaching works best with rhythm, not randomness.

---

### Step 5: Your Sessions
**What to expect:**
- Video call link arrives 24 hours before each session
- Show up as you are — camera on (preferred), pajamas fine, coffee encouraged
- Each session: check-in → deep work → action steps → close
- You'll get a written recap + worksheets within 24 hours after each session
- Between sessions: text/voice check-ins Mon–Fri (I respond within 24 hours)

---

### Step 6: Follow-Up & What's Next
**After your package ends:**
- 30-day check-in call (30 min, free — included in 8-session packages)
- Option to continue with a new package, drop to monthly maintenance sessions ($150/session), or graduate with your full toolkit
- Testimonial request (only if you're comfortable — never required)

## Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

Life happens — on both sides. Here's how we handle it, with grace and boundaries:

**Rescheduling:**
- **More than 24 hours notice** → free, no questions asked. Just rebook.
- **Under 24 hours notice** → three-strike system (goes both ways):
  - Strikes 1 & 2: grace, no penalty. Life is life — kids get sick, dogs have emergencies, things come up.
  - **Strike 3 (you cancel):** the session counts as used (you pay for it as a missed session).
  - **Strike 3 (I cancel):** you get a free session added to your package.
- Strikes reset with each new package.

**Why the three-strike system?** Because I'm a human with a life too — I have three kids, an old dog who's my whole world, and real obligations. I extend the same grace I need. But patterns matter, and three times is a pattern.

**Cancelling a package early:**
- **Before Session 1:** full refund, no questions asked. Absolutely.
- **After Session 1 but before Session 2:** refund minus the cost of Session 1 (at the single-session rate) and a $75 admin fee.
- **After Session 2:** sessions are counted individually at the single-session rate (package discount no longer applies). Whatever's left after that math is refunded — or if there's a balance owed, we'll settle it.

**My right to end coaching:**
I reserve the right to discontinue coaching at my discretion — for example, if a client is abusive, racist, threatening, or otherwise creating an unsafe dynamic. If this happens:
- I'll reach out to you directly and tell you what's going on and why.
- You'll be refunded at the per-session cancellation rate (same math as above).
- This isn't about being difficult — it's about keeping this a safe, respectful space for both of us.

**No-shows:**
No-call, no-shows aren't okay. If something comes up, just send a quick text — that's all I ask. Communication goes both ways. An unexplained no-show counts as a strike under the rescheduling policy above. I'm a human, not a corporation, and I just need us to talk to each other.

## Mandated Reporter Disclosure

I'm a mandated reporter in the state of California. That means if, during our work together, I learn about suspected child abuse, neglect, or endangerment — or a threat of serious harm to yourself or someone else — I'm legally required to report it to the appropriate authorities.

This doesn't change the fact that our sessions are a safe, confidential space. It just means there are rare, serious situations where the law requires me to act. I'll always be upfront with you if this comes up.

**What this covers:**
- Suspected child abuse or neglect
- Threats of serious physical harm to yourself or others
- Elder or dependent adult abuse

Everything else stays between us. Period.
