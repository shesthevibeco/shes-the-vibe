## 2. Educational — "Coaching vs. Therapy: what's the difference?"
*Type: educational · Track: all*

**Caption draft:**
"Should I get a therapist or a coach?" — I get this question a lot, so let's clear it up. No shame in either. Sometimes you need both.

**Therapy** = a licensed mental health professional. They diagnose, they treat, they go deep into the past to heal it. If you're dealing with trauma, depression, anxiety — start there.

**Coaching** = forward-looking. We take where you are RIGHT NOW and build where you're going — with real tools, real strategy, and someone in your corner keeping you accountable.

I'm a certified coach (NLP + positive parenting), not a licensed therapist — and I'll always tell you straight which one you actually need.

Think of it like this: therapy helps you understand the wound. Coaching helps you build the life after it.
