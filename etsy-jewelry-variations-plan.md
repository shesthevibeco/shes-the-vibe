## Proposed variation structure

**Variation type:** `Style` (single variation type — cleanest for one-of-a-kind pieces)

| Variation option | Price | Qty | SKU (suggested) | Variation image |
|------------------|-------|-----|-----------------|-----------------|
| 1 - Copper Wire Cuff, Turquoise | $38.00 | 1 | STV-BR-01-COP-TURQ | copper-turquoise collage |
| 2 - Gold Double Strand, Turquoise & Crystal | $40.00 | 1 | STV-BR-02-GLD-DBL | green-tennis collage |
| 3 - Pearl & Copper, Mushroom Charms | $38.00 | 1 | STV-BR-03-PRL-MUSH | pearl-mushroom collage |
| 4 - Copper Wave, Green Aventurine | $38.00 | 1 | STV-BR-04-COP-WAVE | wave collage |
| 5 - Gold Toggle, Turquoise Beads | $38.00 | 1 | STV-BR-05-GLD-TOG | toggle collage |

**Pricing notes:**
- $38 uniform keeps it simple. Style #2 (double strand + crystal) has the most materials/labor — $40 is justified if Christina wants to differentiate. Her call.
- Quantity = 1 each: these are one-of-a-kind handmade pieces. Etsy will show "Only 1 available" which creates urgency. When one sells, she deactivates that variation option (or marks qty 0) rather than deleting the listing.
- SKUs are optional but recommended for her own tracking as the collection grows.

**Why only ONE variation type:** Max is 2, but a second type (e.g. Size) adds confusion for one-of-a-kind pieces. Keep it to Style only. If she later offers the same design in multiple sizes, add "Size" as type 2 then.

## Step-by-step: Set up in Etsy Shop Manager

Christina — do this on desktop (etsy.com/shop/ShesTheVibe), not the app. Takes ~15 min.

### Part 1: Add the variation

1. Shop Manager → **Listings** → find the bracelet listing → click the **pencil/edit** icon.
2. Scroll to the **Variations** section → click **"Add variations."**
3. For variation type, select **"Style"** (if "Style" isn't offered, choose "Custom" and type `Style`).
4. Add the 5 options, exactly:
   - `1 - Copper Wire Cuff, Turquoise`
   - `2 - Gold Double Strand, Turquoise & Crystal`
   - `3 - Pearl & Copper, Mushroom Charms`
   - `4 - Copper Wave, Green Aventurine`
   - `5 - Gold Toggle, Turquoise Beads`
   - (Numbering them 1–5 matches the photo order and makes buyer messages unambiguous: "I want #3!")
5. Click **"Add variation."** Etsy generates the 5 combinations.

### Part 2: Price, quantity, SKU per option

6. In the variations table, set for each row:
   - **Price:** $38.00 (or $40.00 for option 2 if you want the premium)
   - **Quantity:** 1
   - **SKU:** STV-BR-01 through STV-BR-05 (optional but useful)
7. Make sure "Quantities" and "Prices vary" toggles are ON.

### Part 3: Assign variation images

8. Still in the variations table, click the **image icon** on each row → select that style's collage photo.
   - Row 1 → copper-turquoise collage
   - Row 2 → green-tennis collage
   - Row 3 → pearl-mushroom collage
   - Row 4 → wave collage
   - Row 5 → toggle collage
9. Now when a buyer picks from the dropdown, the main photo swaps to their piece. This is the "labeled picture" behavior.

### Part 4: Update text

10. **Description:** paste the "PICK YOUR PIECE" block at the very top (see above).
11. **Title:** swap to the proposed title (see above).
12. Scroll up, click **Publish/Save.**

### Part 5: Verify

13. Open the live listing in a private/incognito window.
14. Confirm the **Style dropdown** appears with all 5 numbered options.
15. Select each option — confirm the photo swaps and the price is right.

## When a bracelet sells

- Set that variation's **quantity to 0** (don't delete the option — sold-out options show as unavailable, which is social proof).
- If Christina makes a replacement in the same style, restock to 1 and upload fresh photos if the new piece looks different.
- If she adds a NEW style: edit listing → Variations → "Add an option" → upload its collage → set price/qty.

## Future consideration

If the collection grows past ~8 styles, split into 2 listings (e.g. "Copper Collection" and "Gold Collection") — Etsy's variation UI gets clunky beyond 8-10 options, and two listings = double the search visibility.
