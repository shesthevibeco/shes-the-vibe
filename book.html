A novel by Christina Wood

The Girl Who Burned Her Mother’s Blueprint

Coming soon — join the list to be first to read it.

Every family hands you a blueprint — who to be, how to behave, which parts of yourself are acceptable and which ones to bury. For some of us, that blueprint was drawn by someone who needed us small.

The Girl Who Burned Her Mother’s Blueprint is the story of a girl who decides to set hers on fire — and build something of her own from the ashes.

It’s about the mother wound, and what grows in the space where it used to live. About learning the difference between obedience and love. About the terrifying, gorgeous moment you realize the life you were given was never the life you were meant for — and you get to choose again.

This is the book I’ve been writing my whole life. The journals, the blog posts, the brand — they were all rehearsal. This is the main stage.

Practical. Pretty. You. ♥
