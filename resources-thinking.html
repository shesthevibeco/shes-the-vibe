The shelf that raised me

What Changed My Thinking

Books, shows, voices, and ideas that cracked my thinking wide open — and might do the same for you.

Books

Ellen Hopkins

All of them. Yes, the YA novels in verse about addiction and trauma and girls surviving impossible things. Unconventional, I know — but nobody writes the truth about pain like she does.

Beqoming: Everything You Didn’t Know You Wanted

By Azrya + Benjamin Bequer. The title says it all.

Atomic Habits

By James Clear. Tiny changes, remarkable results — the book that taught me growth doesn’t have to be dramatic to be real.

Untamed

By Glennon Doyle. Stop the pleasing, start the living — and trust the knowing inside you.

Daring Greatly

By Brené Brown. On vulnerability, shame, and showing up anyway.

The Body Keeps the Score

By Bessel van der Kolk. How trauma lives in the body — and how it can finally leave.

The Four Agreements

By Don Miguel Ruiz. Four simple agreements that quietly rewired how I move through the world.

Watch

Bluey

Shocker, it’s good. A kids’ cartoon that quietly teaches you how to be a better parent and a softer human. Watch it with your kids. Watch it without them.

This Is Us

Generational trauma, family love, and healing — in the most beautifully devastating packaging.

Ted Lasso

Believe. Kindness as a strategy, softness as strength.

Insecure

Issa Rae’s love letter to Black women figuring it out — awkward, brilliant, and real.

Teachers

Tony Robbins

The big energy, the uncomfortable questions, the refusal to let you stay small.

Dave Ramsey

The financial guru. Debt is dumb, cash is king — the money mindset reset I didn’t know I needed.

Listen

For the parenting journey…

Good Inside with Dr. Becky

Practical scripts for the hardest parenting moments — from clinical psychologist Dr. Becky Kennedy.

No Guilt Mom

Parenting without the shame spiral. Practical advice for moms, minus the guilt trip.

Peace and Parenting

How to parent without punishments, yelling, or shame — and actually enjoy your kids.

Authentic Parenting

Trauma-informed parenting: break generational cycles by doing your own healing work first.

Respectful Parenting: Janet Lansbury Unruffled

The classic. Calm, respectful approaches to the chaos of raising small humans.

For becoming her…

The Mel Robbins Podcast

No-nonsense mindset shifts and the push you need to stop waiting and start moving.

Girl Just Heal

A soft place to land — healing at your own pace, feeling your feelings, becoming whole.

Brown Girl Self-Care

Self-care without guilt, from Bre Mitchell — for women learning to choose themselves.

This shelf keeps growing. What changed your thinking? Tell me about it through the contact page.
