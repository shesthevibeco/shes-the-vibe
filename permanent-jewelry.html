Questions, Answered

Does the weld hurt?

No. The weld is a quick, tiny spark that joins the chain’s jump ring — a protective barrier sits between the chain and your skin, and the weld never touches your skin. Most people feel nothing at all.

How long does an appointment take?

The fitting and weld itself takes only a few minutes once your chain is ready — most of the fun is choosing your fit and your charms. Because your piece is custom-ordered first, we’ll confirm the details of your appointment when you book.

What if I need to remove it?

Easy — snip the chain at the welded ring with a pair of scissors and it comes right off. Keep your chain: it can be re-welded back on later.

What about airports and TSA?

Fine jewelry generally doesn’t need to be removed for security screening, and permanent jewelry is fine jewelry — you can fly in it, no problem.

What about an MRI or a medical procedure?

Always tell your provider you’re wearing permanent jewelry — some procedures may require it to come off. If it does, it snips off in seconds and can be welded right back on afterward.

Can I shower, swim, and work out in it?

Yes — it’s made for everyday wear: showers, pools, workouts, all of real life. Like any fine jewelry, give it a gentle clean now and then and be kind to it around harsh chemicals; gold-filled and sterling pieces reward a little basic care with a long, shiny life.

Can kids get permanent jewelry?

Yes — kids’ pieces are available with a parent present; your child just needs to be able to sit still for the quick weld.

What if my chain breaks?

Don’t panic, and don’t throw it away. If your weld pops within 60 days, your re-weld is free. After that, re-welds are $15 — keep your chain and reach out, and we’ll get you back on.
