The woman behind the vibe

Hi, I’m Christina

As a kid, I was oppressed by my mother. I learned early how to dim my vibe to keep her happy — to make myself smaller, quieter, easier to love.

She resented it whenever I wanted to explore different things, try new lanes, be more than one box. For years, I shrank myself to fit inside someone else’s comfort.

Then one day I stopped apologizing for it. I’m not one vibe — I fit in so many different ones. I’m a planner and a dreamer, soft and disciplined, creative and practical. That’s not confusion. That’s the whole point.

She’s The Vibe is the space I never had growing up — where every woman gets to be vibrantly, loudly herself without dimming for anybody. Our motto says it all: “Practical. Pretty. You.”

I’m a Black mom building this from Silverado, California, under my company Wood Family Creations LLC. I’m also writing a book about my journey — because this story is bigger than products. It’s permission. And I want every woman who finds us to feel it.

Practical. Pretty. You. ♥
