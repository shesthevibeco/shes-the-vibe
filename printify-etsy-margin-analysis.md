## 🚨 Issues Found

### 1. Inconsistent variant pricing (needs fixing)
- **Sweatshirt White/XL** is listed at **$62.88** while other colors are $75.60 — same $37.73 cost. That's leaving $13 on the table.
- **Hoodie Cherry Red/M** is listed at **$68.35** while others are $74.70 — same $41.01 cost. Inconsistent.

### 2. Tote margin is thin (28%)
At $25.99 with a $15.97 cost, you're only keeping $7.30 after Etsy fees. Consider bumping to **$29.99** → profit becomes ~$10.80 (36% margin).

### 3. Digital products = pure profit ✅
Your 13 digital listings ($9–$37) have zero production cost. After Etsy fees (~10%), you're keeping ~90%. These are your margin heroes.

### 4. Handmade goods (not in Printify)
- Waist beads ($15), soap ($15), bracelet ($28) — these are your labor + materials. Make sure you're pricing your time in.
