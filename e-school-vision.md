# The Vibe School — Vision Scaffold

*Prepared 2026-10-10. Christina's words: "e-school for a wide magnitude of things." This is the map — she picks the first road. Nothing built, nothing bought.*

## The idea in one line

A She's The Vibe online school where women learn the *systems behind the products* — planning, parenting, healing, spiritual practice — taught in her voice, mostly faceless.

## Why the timing works

- She already owns the curriculum skeleton: **8 guided journals = 8 course workbooks**, **5 spreadsheets = 5 systems courses**, and her book manuscript is the signature story spine.
- She already holds the credentials to teach it: Certified Positive Parenting Coach (#26-00948) + NLP Practitioner / Life Coach.
- The vault topic analysis found her biggest zero-coverage gaps (shadow work, manifestation system, chakras, ancestral healing) — those are *premium course topics*, not just journal topics.

## Pillars — each maps to something she already owns

1. **Parent Like They're People** — credential: Positive Parenting Coach. Workbook-ready: Healing Vibe (boundaries, inner child). The site already carries the philosophy ("Your Child Is A Whole Person").
2. **The Vibe Systems** — assets: Vibe Dashboard, Bills Babe, Work Vibe, Home Care Vibe. Course pitch: "get your life together in a weekend." Natural upsell from the $37 dashboard.
3. **Stop Dimming Your Light** — the signature course. Assets: book manuscript + Healing Vibe. Founder-story-driven; nobody can copy her lived narrative.
4. **Spiritual Practice, Taught Properly** — assets: Read the Energy, Dream Vibe. The vault's Tier 1/2 gaps (shadow work, manifestation, moon cycles, chakras) become course modules.
5. **Faith Study** — assets: Faith Vibe (124 pp) + Ethiopian expansion. Bible-study course in an underserved niche.
6. **Reading Life** — assets: Reading Vibe journals. Lighter lift; book-club/community energy.

## MVP — pick ONE first (her call)

- **Option A: Stop Dimming Your Light** — signature, most on-brand, hardest to copy.
- **Option B: Parent Like They're People** — credential-backed, clear buyer pain, real differentiator.
- **Option C: Vibe Systems Weekend** — fastest to build; the products already exist, it's screen recordings + spreadsheets she owns.

Lean: **C first** (fastest revenue, proves the model), then **A** as the flagship. But it's her decision.

## Format — faceless-friendly by design

- Screen recordings (dashboard walkthroughs), slide decks + voiceover, PDF workbooks (already made — zero extra production).
- The tts skill can draft voiceover audio from her scripts; she reviews everything in her voice before anything ships.
- Start async/self-paced (fits a full-time job + kids). Live Q&A can come later.

## Platform — decide later, not now

- Hosted (Teachable / Thinkific / Podia): fastest, monthly fee.
- Self-hosted course pages on shesthevibe.co: everything in-house, more build.
- No decision needed until the MVP is picked.

## How it connects to what's already built

- **Vibe Vault** (template/content packs) = the low-ticket library. **The school** = the high-ticket guided layer. Same topics, deeper container — they stack instead of competing.
- Every course ships with its journal as the workbook. Products she already sells become course materials at zero extra cost.
- Built documented and clean, per her rule: "when I get bigger I can have someone else manage it with their Muse."
