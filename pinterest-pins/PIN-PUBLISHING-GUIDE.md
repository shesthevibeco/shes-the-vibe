## Publishing Tips
- **Best times:** Evenings 8-11pm and weekends perform best on Pinterest
- **Frequency:** Pin 3-5 per day, not all at once — spread them out
- **Boards:** Create boards for each category (Journals, Planners, Handmade, Coaching, Free Resources, Inspiration)
- **Keywords:** Pinterest is a search engine — the titles and descriptions above are SEO-optimized, don't shorten them
- **Fresh pins:** Pinterest favors new images — these are all fresh, never posted before
