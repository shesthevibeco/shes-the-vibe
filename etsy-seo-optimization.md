3. **Missing the hottest niches** — `ADHD planner` (10k+ review sellers), `shadow work`, `bible study`, `tarot spread`, and `budget planner` are the top-converting searches in these categories and were absent from titles.
4. **Work Vibe, Home Care Vibe, Bills Babe had no SEO drafts** — published without optimized titles/tags. These are now drafted from scratch.
