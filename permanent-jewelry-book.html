Start Here — How Booking Works

Four steps and you’re booked. Most people finish steps 1–3 in about ten minutes.

1. Read the Packet & Release

The Before Your Appointment packet covers how it works, pricing, travel, and setup. The Release & Waiver gets signed before appointment day — zero paperwork energy at your appointment.

Before Your Appointment (PDF) Release & Waiver (PDF)

2. Send Your Booking Request

Fill out the booking request form below — your piece, your chain, your metal, your city. Going custom? Drop your inspo pic link in the form, or email the picture itself to christina@shesthevibe.co.

Go to the Form

3. Start With Your Deposit

Your deposit is what lets me order your chain and materials — $25 for Standards, $75 for Custom Elite, both credited toward your total. Book your spot and pay it right in my booking calendar.

See Deposit Options

4. I Order, Confirm & Come to You

Once your chain is in, I’ll confirm your final quote and appointment details. Then I show up with everything — all I need from you is a small work area and a power outlet (no outlet? I can run it from my car).
