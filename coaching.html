What Coaching With Me Looks Like

Life Coaching

1:1 Coaching · For Anyone Ready to Shine

I spent years dimming myself to keep everyone else comfortable — and I coach people who are done doing the same. Together we untangle the roles, the guilt, and the “shoulds” so you can build a life that actually feels like yours. Every session is real talk, real strategy, and zero judgment.

NLP-Informed Sessions

Rewire the Patterns · Change the Story

As a certified NLP Practitioner, I help you spot the thought loops and old scripts running your life on autopilot — and rewrite them. We work with the language of your mind: the beliefs you inherited, the fears you rehearsed, and the version of you that’s been waiting underneath all of it.

Positive Parenting Coaching

Whole-Person Parenting · For Parents Who Want Better

I’m a certified Positive Parenting Coach and a mom of three — I know the trenches. My philosophy: children are whole people with their own voices — and that goes for moms, dads, and every caregiver showing up. Together we’ll build connection over control, repair over punishment, and a calmer home where everyone (including you) gets to be human.

Divorce & Separation Support

Lived Experience · For Families Finding Their Way Through

I’ve been through a messy divorce and navigated the whole court system — so I know how lonely, overwhelming, and disorienting it feels. This is a space to process the grief, keep the kids at the center, and find a way through that protects everyone’s peace. We’ll work on co-parenting with grace, setting boundaries that hold, and helping your family unit heal — or separate as peacefully as possible.

This is coaching and emotional support, not legal advice.

Who Coaching Is For

This is for the person who looks like they have it all together — and is quietly exhausted. The parent who wants to parent differently than they were parented. The dreamer who keeps shrinking themselves to fit rooms they were never meant to stay in. Whoever you are — you’re welcome here.

The Overfunctioner

You hold everything together for everyone else and fall apart quietly. We’ll work on boundaries that stick, guilt that quiets, and a life where your needs count too.

The Cycle-Breaker

You grew up dimming yourself to keep the peace — and you refuse to pass that on. We’ll rewrite the old scripts and build a new blueprint, one brave choice at a time.

The Conscious Parent

You want to parent with connection, not control — but the old patterns flare up. Whether you’re a mom, a dad, or doing it solo, we’ll build tools for the hard moments and care for the parent behind the parenting.

The One Going Through It

Divorce or separation is turning your world upside down — and you refuse to let it turn your kids’ world upside down too. Whether you’re a mom or a dad, we’ll navigate the grief, keep things civil, and protect your family’s peace through the storm.
